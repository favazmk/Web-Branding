How we work

The edit is decided before the camera moves

A shoot booked without knowing what it has to become is the most expensive way to end up with footage nobody uses.

Formats first

We agree the deliverables — how many cuts, at what ratios, at what lengths — before a call sheet exists. Framing on the day then leaves room for the vertical crop instead of forcing it later, which is why so much repurposed footage looks wrong.

Your brand, not a preset

Grades, titles, captions and end cards are built from the palette and typeface the brand already uses. Footage is where most identities come apart; it should look like the same company that made the feed post.

Shot for the second view

Enough coverage, b-roll and cutaways are captured to recut the same material for a new campaign without going back to set. One production day should keep paying out for months, not expire with the campaign it was booked for.
