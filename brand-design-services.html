Why it matters

People judge the logo
before they read the sentence

Design is not decoration applied at the end. It is the first thing a customer meets — on a shopfront, a delivery box, a search result, or a reel that plays for two seconds before the thumb moves on. Get it wrong and every other thing you spend money on has to work harder to make up for it.

So we design the system rather than the artwork: the mark, the type it sits with, the colours it lives in, and the rules that keep it recognisable whether it is printed on a van or cropped into a profile picture.

How We Work

From questions to guidelines

01

Discovery

We learn the business, the customer, and the competitors before anything is drawn. A session of questions saves three rounds of revisions later.

02

Directions

Two or three genuinely different concepts, each with the reasoning behind it. You choose a direction rather than a favourite colour.

03

Refinement

The chosen route is drawn out across real applications — sign, card, phone screen, packaging — and revised with you until it holds up everywhere.

04

Guidelines & Handover

Source files, every export format, and a guidelines document — so whoever you hire next already knows the rules.

Handover

What lands in your folder

A design project that leaves you dependent on the designer is a bad design project. Whatever route you take, this is what you own at the end of it.

Editable source files

The working Illustrator, Figma, or After Effects files — not just flattened images. They are yours.

Every export format

AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, PNG and JPG at print and screen resolution, named so you can find them.

Brand guidelines

Clearances, minimum sizes, colour values, type rules, and the things nobody is allowed to do to the logo.

Editable social templates

A template set your team can fill in themselves, so routine posts do not need a designer.

Print-ready artwork

CMYK, bleed, and outlined type, checked with the printer's requirements rather than sent and hoped for.

Web & profile pack

Favicons, app icons, and correctly cropped profile and cover art for every platform you use.

Developer-ready handoff

Figma with components, spacing, and tokens, so the build matches the design instead of approximating it.

Support after handover

A window of included support for resizes, new formats, and the applications nobody thought of on day one.
